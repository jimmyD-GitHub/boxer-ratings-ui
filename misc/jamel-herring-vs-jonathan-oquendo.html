Jamel Herring

WBO Super Featherweight World Title Fight

The Bubble, MGM Grand, Las Vegas - July 14th | July 15th 2AM in the UK

Jamel Herring (21-2) makes the 2nd defence of his WBO Super Featherweight title in Top Rank's biggest behind-closed-doors fight to date. His opponent is former WBA Featherweight world title challenger Jonathan Oquendo (31-6). Herring captured the title from Masayuki Ito of Japan in May 2019 and went on to make a successful first defence against Lamont Roach in November.

Herring boxed beautifully throughout both of those contests. Ito and Roach were unable to cope with the rangy southpaw's fluid right jab and were reguarly picked off with straight left hands to the body and head. Herring is a superb judge of timing and distance. He has made significant improvements since dropping a 10 round unanimous decision to Ladarius Miller in 2017.

Oquendo has been a leading contender at Super Bantamweight and Featherweight for much of the past decade but at 36 years of age the best days of his career seem firmly behind him. The Puerto Rican lost to Lamont Roach by unanimous decision over 10 rounds in May 2019 but bounced back with a shut out of Charles Huerta 6 months later.

Oquendo is an orthodox boxer/puncher who jabs well with the left hand and is capable of eye catching combinations when he gets his opponent on the back foot. The left hook to the body is a potent weapon, as is his right cross. Oquendo kept Roach under pressure but had to take a lot of counter right hands during their fight. Herring basically dominated Roach from long range on his way to a convincing victory, which tells it's own story.

Oquendo will be giving away a daunting 6 inches in height when he faces Herring and is also at a 3 inch reach disadvantage. The challenger is likely to have great difficulty getting inside Herring's jab. On top of this, Oquendo hasn't always looked too comfortable against southpaws. He was very open to right hooks when losing inside 3 rounds to Juan Manual Lopez in Puerto Rico, although that was way back in 2008.

Betting on the contest opened with Herring a big 18/1 on (-1800) favourite here in the UK. The champion is not considered a big puncher, with less than 50% of his victories coming inside schedule. Oquendo is a battle hardened, durable veteran who hasn't failed to go the distance in a bout since 2012. Herring by unanimous decision (4/9 or -225) would appear to be one of the safer world title fight predictions that can be made this year.
