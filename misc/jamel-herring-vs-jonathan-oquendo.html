Jamel Herring

WBO Super Featherweight World Title Fight

The Bubble, MGM Grand, Las Vegas - September 5th | September 6th 2AM in the UK

Jamel Herring ( 21-2 ) makes the 2nd defence of his WBO Super Featherweight title in Top Rank's latest behind-closed-doors main event. His opponent is former WBA Featherweight world title challenger Jonathan Oquendo (31-6 ).

The bout has been scheduled to go ahead on two previous occasions but twice had to be postponed due to Herring testing positive for Covid-19. Hopefully the champion is well enough to box this time around.

Herring captured the title from Japan's Masayuki Ito in May 2019 before making a successful first defence against Lamont Roach last November. The New Yorker boxed beautifully throughout both of those contests.

Ito and Roach were unable to cope with the rangy southpaw's fluid right jab and were reguarly picked off with straight left hands to the body and head. Herring is a superb judge of timing and distance. He has made significant improvements since dropping a 10 round unanimous decision to Ladarius Miller at Lightweight in 2017.

Oquendo has been a leading contender at Super Bantamweight and Featherweight for much of the past decade but at 36 years of age the best days of his career seem firmly behind him. The Puerto Rican lost to Lamont Roach by unanimous decision over 10 rounds in May 2019 but bounced back with a shut out of Charles Huerta 6 months later.

Oquendo is an orthodox boxer/puncher who jabs well with the left hand and is capable of eye catching combinations when he gets his opponent on the back foot. The left hook to the body is a potent weapon, along with his right cross. Oquendo kept Roach under pressure but had to absorb a lot of counter right hands during that bout. Herring largely dominated Roach from long range on his way to a convincing victory, which tells it's own story.

Oquendo will be giving away a daunting 6 inches in height when he faces Herring, who also has a 3 inch reach advantage. Herring is basically a Lightweight who made the decision to boil down to 130lbs after suffering loses to Denis Shafikov and Ladarius Miller at 135lbs. The challenger is likely to have great difficulty avoiding Herring's jab. The champion's southpaw stance may also be a problem for Oquendo, who was very open to right hooks when losing inside 3 rounds to Juan Manual Lopez back in 2008.

Betting on the contest opened with Herring a big 20/1 on (-2000) favourite here in the UK. The champion is not considered a big puncher, with less than 50% of his victories coming inside schedule. Oquendo is a battle hardened, durable veteran who hasn't failed to go the distance in a bout since 2012. Herring by unanimous decision (4/9 or -225) would appear to be one of the safer world title fight predictions that can be made this year.

Victory in this contest could set Herring up for a potentially lucrative bout against former Featherweight and Super Bantamweight world champion Carl Frampton. The man from Northern Ireland is also part of the Top Rank promotional stable.
