Joe Smith Jr

WBO Inter-Continental Light Heavyweight Title Fight

The Bubble, MGM Grand, Las Vegas - August 22nd 2020

Former WBO Light Heavyweight champion Eleider Alvarez ( 25-1 ) takes what he hopes will be another step towards regaining the title when he faces Joe Smith Jr ( 25-3 ) in Las Vegas this weekend. Smith is also campaigning for a second chance at glory, after failing to procure the WBA Light Heavyweight title from Dmitry Bivol in March 2019.

Alvarez became champion by knocking out Sergey Kovalev in August 2018. The Colombian shocked those in attendance by dropping the formidable Russian three times in round 7 to end the fight. His reign as champion did not last long. A rejuvenated Kovalev produced one of the best performances of his career to regain the title via unanimous decision when the pair met in a rematch 6 months later.

Alvarez is not considered a big puncher, but there is a suspicion that he hits a lot harder than his record suggests. His power definitely took Kovalev by surprise in their first bout. Alvarez boxed from the outside and drew leads from Kovalez before countering with fast left hooks and body shots to win the early rounds. Kovalev seemed to have turned the tide when he started timing Alvarez with his jab, but just when it looked like the champion had taken control of the fight Alvarez decked him with a beautifully executed right hand.

Alverez was disappointing in the rematch. Kovalev was able to pick up where he left off before being dropped in the first fight. The Russian controlled his opponent from long range with his jab throughout the contest.

Alvarez seemed oddly tentative, lacking the intensity and purpose he'd shown the night he won the title. Joe Smith Jr will be hoping he encounters the Alvarez we saw in the rematch with Kovlev. It was a much sharper version of the Colombian who became champion.

Alvarez was back to something like his best when knocking out Michael Seals in his last ring appearance. Alvarez established his jab early in the contest and used it to set Seals up for a fight ending right cross in the 7th round. The jab really is the key for Alvarez, all of his best work comes when he is able to measure opponents with his left hand.

Smith burst onto the Light Heavyweight world scene in 2016 with KO wins over Andrzej Fonfara and the legendary Bernard Hopkins. The American would certainly appear to be the heavier handed of the two. Twenty of Smith's 25 victories have come inside schedule, against just 13 quick wins for Alvarez.

Smith dropped Fonfara heavily in the opening round with a massive right hand then overwhelmed the Polish fighter with a barrage of hooks and uppercuts. Hopkins was a month shy of his 52nd birthday when he faced Smith in the final bout of his career but was still largely favoured to win. Smith was having none of it. He got stuck into the veteran from the opening bell and ended the fight in round 8 when a hard right hand caused Hopkins to fall out of the ring.

Since those breakthrough performances Smith has suffered unanimous decision losses to Sullivan Barrera and Dmitry Bivol. Smith showed great determination and heart in both contests but lacked the variation and craft needed to overcome such accomplished boxers.

Smith showed his power and toughness against Barrera. He floored the Cuban with a sweeping left hook in the opening round and seemed to be on his way to victory. Smith might well have defeated Barrera had he not been hampered by a broken jaw sustained in the 2nd round. Few fighters would have boxed on for 8 rounds with such a bad injury, but the New Yorker is a real hard man.

The outstanding Bivol used his superb footwork and punch selection to prevent Smith from pushing the champion back to the ropes. Smith is less effective when he cannot back his opponent up. However, he never stopped trying to win against Bivol and did hurt the Russian badly with a right hook at the end of the 10th round.

In his last ring appearance Smith out worked and out punched Jesse Hart over 10 rounds to get back to winning ways. That experience will stand him in good stead for the fight with Alvarez. Hart is a talented boxer with good movement, but Smith kept him under pressure and never allowed his opponent to find a rhythm. Hart visited the canvas in round 7 after taking a flush right hand to the head while trapped on the ropes.

Smith vs Alvarez has the makings of a pleasing stylistic match up. Smith always looks to come forward and causes problems with his raw strength, work rate and punching power. Alvarez will be happy to concede the centre of the ring to Smith. The Colombian will jab from distance while circling his opponent, looking for opportunities to throw fast combinations or openings for a hard right cross. The challenge for Smith is to find ways of cutting the ring off so that he can land heavy punches on Alvarez when his back is to the ropes.

So the outcome really depends on which fighter is able to assert their style of boxing more effectively. It seems unlikely that the fight will not last the full 12 rounds. Neither man has been floored or stopped during their professional career. The odds makers favour the Colombian at 1/2 (-200), with Smith at 6/4 (+150). Alvarez has the more eye catching names on his resume and it wouldn't come as a surprise if he proved too classy and too technically adept for the less polished Smith.

However, I believe that Smith has the tenacity, strength and desire to spring a mild upset. The Kovalev rematch showed that Alvarez can become quite passive and disheartened when a contest is not going his way. If Smith starts fast and is prepared to make the fight a punishing, physical affair I think he can dissuade Alvarez from throwing punches. There will be times when Alvarez out jabs and out boxes Smith but I think he will have to do most of his fighting off the back foot.

Smith has spent large parts of his career trying to overcome more technically gifted opponents and on this occasion, I think he will be successful. I'm backing the man from Long Island to previal via a hotly contested split decision verdict.
