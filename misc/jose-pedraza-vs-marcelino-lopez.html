Jose Pedraza

10 Round Light Welterweight Non-Title Fight

MGM Grand, Las Vegas - July 2nd 2020 | July 3rd 3AM in the UK

Former WBO Lightweight champion Jose Pedraza (26-3 ) will be hoping to build some momentum at 140lbs when he faces Marcelino Lopez (36-2-1 ) on Top Rank's latest behind closed doors show. Lopez is a late replacement for Mikkel LesPierre, who was forced out of the event when his manager tested positive for Covid-19.

Pedraza captured the title with a unanimous decision victory over Ray Beltran in August 2018 but lost it to P4P star Vasiliy Lomachenko in a unification bout 4 months later.

Pedraza boxed beautifully against Beltran in the stand out performance of his career. "Sniper" is a switch hitter who seems equally comfortable throwing combinations from either stance. He dropped Beltran with a well-timed southpaw left uppercut in the 11th round. The Puerto Rican has never failed to go the distance during his professional career and has only been floored by outstanding fighters like Lomachenko and Gervonta Davis.

Pedraza gave a good account of himself when taking Lomachenko the full 12 rounds, depite losing by a wide margin on the scorecards. Since that defeat his performances have been less convincing. Pedraza was given a tough examination by Mexican Antonio Lozada in May 2019 but prevailed via 9th round TKO. In September 2019 he was thoroughly out boxed and outpunched by leading 140lb contender Jose Zepeda.

In both of those contests his balance and speed appeared slightly diminished. Observers will be looking for indicators as to whether the 31 year old has become a fighter on the way down or is simply in the process of adjusting to a new weight class.

If Pedraza is vulnerable then he is at greater risk of being exposed by Lopez than he would have been against LesPierre. LesPierre challenged for a world title against Maurice Hooker in 2019 but Lopez has generally faced a higher level of competition. "Nino" scored a 2nd round TKO win over Pablo Cesar Cano in 2017 and followed it up with a 5th round knockout of Breidis Prescott in 2018.

The Argentine is a compact, methodical boxer who liked to push forward behind a high guard. In all of his stoppage victories he has jabbed and hooked to the body with his left hand to set up a flashing overhand right to the head. The punch seems to be a potent weapon for Lopez. Cesar Cano could not recover after being dropped with the right hand and Prescott was down twice from the same punch before that fight was waved off.

Lopez has never been knocked down during his professional career. The only two boxers to defeat him, Pablo Barboza and Michael Perez, both had to get off the canvas to earn decision victories. The Argentine appears to be strong and durable and is far more experienced than Pedraza at the 140lb weight limit.
