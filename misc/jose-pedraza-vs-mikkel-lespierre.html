Jose Pedraza

10 Round Light Welterweight Non-Title Fight

MGM Grand, Las Vegas - July 2nd 2020 | July 3rd 3AM in the UK

Former WBO Lightweight champion Jose Pedraza (26-3 ) will be hoping to build some momentum at 140lbs in Top Rank's latest Vegas show. The Puerto Rican's opponent is one time world title challenger Mikkel LesPierre (22-1-1 ).

Pedraza captured the title with a unanimous decision victory over Ray Beltran in August 2018 but lost it to P4P star Vasiliy Lomachenko in a unification bout 4 months later. LesPierre challenged Maurice Hooker for the WBO Super Lightweight title in March 2019. The southpaw lost by a wide margin on all three scorecards.

Pedraza boxed beautifully against Beltran in the stand out performance of his career. "Sniper" is a switch hitter who seems equally comfortable throwing combinations from either stance. He dropped Beltran with a well-timed southpaw left uppercut in the 11th round. The Puerto Rican has never failed to go the distance during his professional career and has only been floored by outstanding fighters like Lomachenko and Gervonta Davis.

Pedraza gave a good account of himself when taking Lomachenko 12 rounds, but was only able to win one of them clearly. Since that defeat his performances have been less convincing. Pedraza was given a tough examination by Mexican Antonio Lozada in May 2019 but prevailed via 9th round TKO. In September 2019 he was thoroughly out boxed and outpunched by leading 140lb contender Jose Zepeda.

In both of those contests his balance and speed appeared slightly diminished. Observers will be looking for indications as to whether the 31 year old has become a fighter on the way down, or if he is simply in the process of adjusting to a new weight class.

LesPierre boxed well enough against Hooker , without threatening to win any of the rounds. In by far the biggest fight of his career, LesPierre looked to push forward whenever he could and scored with the occasional right hook. LesPierre is defensively sound and moves well. Given the opportunity he will look to throw combinations to the body and head. Hooker dropped LesPierre in the 9th with a left hook to the body but the New Yorker showed heart and resilience to survive the round.

The bookies make Pedraza a firm favourite at 1/12 (-1200) to win the fight, with good reason. The Puerto Rican has faced a far higher calibre of opposition during his career. Pedraza has been taking on world class boxers for much of the past 3 years while LesPierre has been facing lower level fighters at venues like the BB King Blues Club in New York.

LesPierre might not be the worst long shot we've ever seen. He gave away a 3 inch height advantage to Hooker, who is a rangy, mature Light Welterweight. He won't be at a height disadvantage when he faces Pedraza and he has far more experience at the 140lb weight limit. The 10 round distance could encourage LesPierre to box at a faster pace than he did against Hooker. Sporting types might feel tempted to place a small bet on the underdog at 6/1 (+600).

Ultimately though, the only logical prediction is that Pedraza will record his first win as a Light Welterweight. I feel that the contest will be more competitive than many are expecting. LesPierre might not have the speed, power or variation needed to defeat someone as accomplished as Pedraza but I believe he is good enough to complete 10 rounds.
