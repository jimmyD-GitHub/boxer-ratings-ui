Daniel Dubois

European, Commonwealth and British Heavyweight Title Fight

Church House, Westminster - November 28th 2020

UK boxing fans are on tenterhooks this week in anticipation of the most compelling all British Heavyweight showdown in recent times. Daniel Dubois (15-0 ) and Joe Joyce (11-0 ) will not be competing for a world title this Saturday night, as Frank Bruno did when he challenged Lennox Lewis for the WBC crown at the National Stadium in Cardiff in October 1993, but one has to go back that far to think of a bout between two British big men as significant and exciting as this one.

As recently as December 2015 current WBA, IBF and WBO Heavyweight champion Anthony Joshua met fellow undefeated contender Dillian Whyte for the British and Commonwealth titles at London's O2 Arena. Whyte is now firmly established as a world level Heavyweight but five years ago the Brixton man did not enjoy the same profile Dubois and Joyce share today.
