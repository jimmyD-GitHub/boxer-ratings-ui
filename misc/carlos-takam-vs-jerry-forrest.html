Carlos Takam

10 Round Heavyweight Non-Title Fight

The Bubble, MGM Grand, Las Vegas - July 9th | July 10th 1AM in the UK

Former world title challenger Carlos Takam (38-5-1 ) steps up at short notice to face Jerry Forrest in the main event of Top Rank's latest behind closed doors show. Forrest (26-3 ) had been scheduled to take on Jarell "Big Baby" Miller until the controversial Heavyweight failed yet another drugs test .

Takam gave a good account of himself when challenging WBA and IBF Heavyweight champion Anthony Joshua in October 2017 but lost via 10th round TKO. Forrest lost in consecutive bouts against Gerald Washington and Michael Hunter early in his career but has only been defeated once in 17 contests since then.

Takam is a busy pressure fighter who throws a high number of punches by Heavyweight standards. The Frenchman is at his best when he is able to trap opponents on the ropes. In September 2019 Takam easily out pointed the much taller Craig Lewis by out working and out punching the American in every round. In his last ring appearance Takam was even more dominant when hammering out a 10 round unanimous decision over Fabio Maldonado.

Forrest has been operating at a lower level than Takam but has shown steady improvement during his career. The Virginia southpaw was unlucky to find himself on the wrong side of a 10 round split decision in favour of Jermaine Franklin in July 2019. Forrest out jabbed the undefeated prospect for long periods of the bout and scored regularly with straight left hands.

On paper Takam is a healthy favourite, based on the level of competition he has faced. However, I feel that Forrest is good value at 5/4 (+125) to win. Some sports books in the US actually have Takam as the underdog, with the odds on Forrest narrowing as we draw nearer to fight time.

Takam is likely to start fast but at 39 years of age with only 2 weeks preparation he may struggle to maintain his usual pace. In July 2018 Takam had British contender Derek Chisora under severe pressure in the early stages of a fight at the O2 Arena in London. As the pace of that contest dropped Chisora was able to find the target frequently with right hands, before dropping Takam twice in the 8th round to record a TKO.

Forrest is not as powerful as Chisora but I think this fight could follow a similar pattern. I believe Forrest will find a rhythm with his jab during the middle rounds and will box well enough to overturn an early Takam lead on the cards. Forrest is the younger man by 7 years and this is a huge opportunity for him to impress. I'm backing the home fighter to register the biggest win of his career as the result of a close, possibly contentious, split decision verdict.
