Brad Foster

British And Commonwealth Super Bantamweight Title Fight

BT Sport Studio, Stratford - July 10th 2020

Boxing returns to TV screens in the UK when Brad Foster (12-0-2 ) defends his British and Commonwealth Super Bantamweight titles behind closed doors in London. The bout headlines a 5 fight BT studio event organised by Frank Warren's Queensberry Promotions. Foster's opponent is fellow undefeated Midlander James Beech Jr (12-0 ).

Both men hail from Walsall in England's West Midlands, so local bragging rights are very much on the line. Beech claimed the Midlands Area Featherweight title in 2019 and also won the same title at Super Featherweight. The 23 year old drops down to 122lbs for his first bout at national level.

Beech is a composed, methodical fighter who jabs well from the orthodox stance. He showed good variation in his work when taking the Midlands Area Super Featherweight title from Louis Fielding in 2018. Beech boxed and moved when necessary in that bout but also hurt Fielding with body punching on the inside.

It seemed possible the ringside doctor might call an end to proceedings when referee Shaun Messer invited him to inspect a cut over Beech's left eye in the 9th round. The interruption appeared to instil fresh impetus in Beech, who ended the contest with a series of big right hands a few seconds after the restart.

Beech claimed the Midlands Area Featherweight title after a close fight with Luke Jones in his last ring appearance. Referee Kevin Parker scored the bout 96-95 in Beech's favour. The Bloxwich man was again hindered by a cut sustained over his left eye.

Foster is more experienced at this level despite being the younger man. The 22 year old earned his British title with a unanimous decision over Yorkshireman Josh Wale in March 2019 and followed that up with a 12th round TKO of Commonwealth champion Ashley Lane 2 months later. Foster is a strong, orthodox fighter with good power in both hands. He dropped Lane in the 1st round with a short right hook and again in the final round with a right cross. Foster followed up with an accurate burst of punches to force a dramatic late stoppage.

"The Blade" is a technically correct boxer with sound fundamentals, despite turning professional without the benefit of any amateur experience. In his last ring appearance he produced a career best performance to defeat West Ham's Lucien Reid. The pair had fought to a draw 5 months earlier in September 2019, but Foster was far superior in the rematch. The champion controlled the centre of the ring throughout the contest and found the target repeatedly with straight right hands and flashing left hooks. Reid's corner retired their man after 6 punishing rounds.

The oddsmakers favour Foster at 7/1 on but this is likely to be a competitive, high energy bout. Beech will have to throw more punches than he usually does to gain Foster's respect but I expect him to rise to the occasion. The challenger has been operating in higher weight classes and has a 2-inch height advantage over Foster. If he shows the movement and punch selection he is capable of and stays away from the ropes he may be able to establish an early lead on the scorecards.

Ultimately though, I feel that Foster's punching power and extra experience in 12 round contests will prove decisive. Beech is likely to come under severe pressure from the midpoint onwards and it would come as no surprise if the champion earned a Lonsdale Belt via TKO. Foster showed against Lane what he is capable of late in a fight. I believe he will end this one during the championship rounds.
